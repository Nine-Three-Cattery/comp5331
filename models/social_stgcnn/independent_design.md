# Full-scene Social-STGCNN experiment

Zongmin Zhang, COMP5331 Group 7.

## Question and reference

How accurate are forecasts from the authors' full-scene Social-STGCNN network when it sees only the eight observations available to the group benchmark? The primary architecture follows the operators executed by the authors' `n_stgcnn=1`, `n_txpcnn=5` release. Mohamed et al. specify the graph block, temporal extrapolation, Gaussian prediction and training schedule. The released code resolves the decoder axes, storage reinterpretations and relative-motion graph. The earlier target-readout experiment remains archived at its review tag. This run was specified after that experiment, before fitting the full-scene model. ## Scene construction and training unit

One training example contains all pedestrians with eight consecutive observations in the same recording at the same anchor. Pedestrians occupy ascending numeric ID slots as in the release. Any member without a benchmark future remains visible as an input. Only the targets with twelve existing labels contribute to the loss. Requiring every neighbor to survive the forecast horizon would reveal future information. Singleton scenes are valid benchmark inputs. With one node the normalized Laplacian is zero and the graph block's residual projection carries the observed motion. Labels never determine graph membership or slot order.

For the numerical network, round the neutral positions to four decimal places in double precision, difference them, and cast the increments to float32. Apply target-history corruption or causal filling to the original coordinates before this transform. The first observed displacement is zero. Reciprocal distances between distinct **displacement vectors**, unit self-loops and symmetric degree normalization give each frame's normalized Laplacian. The supervised response uses the same coordinate precision and displacement convention. Predictions integrate from the supplied last target observation in world coordinates. Saved observations and ground truth follow the unrounded group convention.

The learned projection and full graph operator, normalized temporal path and learned residual produce a feature vector for every observed node. The temporal decoder applies its spatial kernels across features and neighboring ID slots before selecting any target. Each trained scene has its actual width. No padded slot participates in a convolution or batch normalization. The scene geometry has no learned parameters and is cached for training. Clean inference can share a scene between targets. Corruption creates a separate graph for each affected target, preserving the other histories.

## Optimization and selection

Use the two-epoch ETH/seed-zero pilot, then 250 SGD epochs per formal fold and seed. SGD has no momentum or gradient clipping and uses a learning rate of 0.01 through epoch 150, then 0.002. The release normalizes activations within each unpadded training scene. Preserve that unit, average the Gaussian likelihood loss over the supervised pedestrians and twelve steps in each scene, and average the gradients of 128 shuffled scenes per update. Include the boundary scene and give the final short batch its actual denominator. The authors' public loop leaves out the scene at an update boundary and divides a partial batch by 128. We implement the minibatch objective itself. The Gaussian density floor is 1e-20. [Released training loop and settings](https://github.com/abduallahmohamed/Social-STGCNN/blob/333d3a57b4d2705e129b21aefefa09c79b2b9ae1/train.py) · [Launcher](https://github.com/abduallahmohamed/Social-STGCNN/blob/333d3a57b4d2705e129b21aefefa09c79b2b9ae1/train.sh)

Select one checkpoint per fold and seed by the lowest clean-validation mean per-target, per-step NLL, breaking ties by earlier epoch. Candidates are epoch 1, every fifth epoch and the last epoch. All six conditions use that same checkpoint. Validation gives each eligible target equal weight. Formal scenes are ETH, Hotel, Univ, Zara1 and Zara2 and training seeds are 0, 1 and 2. These settings and the published full-scene architecture specify this run independently of its held-out scores. [Configuration](configs/experiment.json)

The known-distance fixture checks the owner's ADE and FDE calculation independently of the group evaluator. Formal reporting uses the six `causal_v2` conditions and 20 draws per target. The same random stream is used across conditions. Score within each holdout and seed, average seeds and give the five holdouts equal weight. Report best/mean displacement errors, sample diversity, Energy Score, motion, turning, and contemporaneous pair proximity. The two previously fixed clean window IDs and the seed-zero maximum-clean-minADE rule are retained to support comparison with the archived variant. The separate matched ETH example follows the group handoff. Differences between the two architectures describe the systems as tested because several operators and the supervision unit changed together.

Measure 128 registered targets in batches of 32, three warmups and ten repetitions on one physical Linux CPU node for all fifteen checkpoints.
