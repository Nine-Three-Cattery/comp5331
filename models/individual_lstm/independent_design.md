# Design frozen before the formal run

Observation: eight meter-space xy positions. Use seven within-window displacements; no maps, neighbor features, track derivatives, future positions or learned normalization. Encoder Linear(2,16)+ReLU and one hidden-32 LSTM; autoregressive LSTMCell decoder emits twelve displacements through a linear head. Accumulate displacements and add the last visible/causally filled position. K=1. CV extrapolates the final observed displacement. No teacher forcing or pretraining.

Loss: mean over windows and steps of squared Euclidean position error. Adam 0.001, batch 512, gradient norm clip 1.0, at most 40 epochs. Select on clean validation ADE with improvement greater than 0.00001 m; retain earlier incumbent on ties/near-ties, stop after 8 stale epochs. No test labels in fit. Two-epoch separate ETH pilot, then new weights for all five folds and seeds 0/1/2; no search or tuning between stages. CPU only, zero GPU time needed. Script checks future/neighbor invariance and metric round-trip in own_unit_tests/test_causal.py. Forecasting uses shared corruption first, model-local differencing second. No stochastic inference: the recorded sampling_seed is the training seed and generates no forecast randomness.
