\section{Research question}
How do forecast error, sample coverage and robustness trade off when six
independent models receive the same histories? We use eight observed and
twelve future positions at 0.4\,s intervals on five ETH/UCY holdouts
\cite{pellegrini2009,lerner2007}. Models share ordered windows, three
training seeds and validation-only checkpoint selection.

\section{Reading the evidence}
ADE averages displacement over time. FDE measures endpoint displacement.
Report one path for deterministic models, or minimum and mean errors over
twenty samples for probabilistic models. Energy Score averages the RMS
trajectory distance from each draw to the observed future, then subtracts
half the mean RMS distance over pairs of distinct draw indices
\cite{gneiting2007}. The pairwise term is zero for \(K=1\).
Folds receive equal weight after averaging seeds.

We perturb target history with 0.05, 0.10 or 0.20\,m Gaussian position noise,
or remove the last one or two target observations and extrapolate from the
last two visible points. Neighbor histories and future labels stay fixed.
Timing uses 128 targets with batch size 32, three warmups and ten
measurements, from in-memory neutral positions to CPU predictions.
