\subsection*{Zongmin Zhang}
I implemented the full-scene Social-STGCNN model and its interface to the group benchmark. An earlier version reduced each frame to a single target vector. I replaced that decoder after comparing it with the authors' implementation. My work covers the displacement graph, spatial and temporal convolutions, likelihood, sampling, training and export. I checked that every eligible target kept its original position in the scene and that future observations could not influence its inputs. I ran the ETH pilot and five-fold, three-seed experiment, checked the selected weights, and prepared the accuracy, robustness, crowd-size, motion and timing analyses. I also prepared the figures, report section and presentation. I used generative AI assistance for interpreting sources, implementation, testing, experiment execution, analysis and drafting.
