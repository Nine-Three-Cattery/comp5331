\subsection*{Zongmin Zhang}
I was responsible for the Social-STGCNN component of the group comparison. My contribution covers the graph construction, temporal network, Gaussian loss and sampling, and the interface to the shared ETH/UCY benchmark. I retained every pedestrian with eight visible positions after inspecting the crowd sizes in the training data. Caching the geometry made that choice practical during training. I ran the ETH pilot and the five-fold, three-seed experiment, selected checkpoints on clean validation trajectories, and prepared an independent check of the selected weights. I also prepared the per-scene results, robustness and crowd analyses, runtime measurements, trajectory figures, report section and presentation. I used generative AI assistance for paper interpretation, implementation, testing, experiment execution, analysis and drafting. The source inventory, predictions and review receipts document the work underlying these materials.
