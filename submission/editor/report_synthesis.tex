\paragraph{Clean forecasts.}
The supplementary last-step constant-velocity baseline achieved
0.534\,m ADE, below both learned \(K=1\) models. At \(K=20\), Social-GAN
had the lowest minimum ADE, Trajectron++ had the lowest minimum FDE and
Energy Score, and DSPIGCN had the lowest mean ADE and mean FDE. The paired
Trajectron++ minus Social-GAN minimum ADE difference was \(-0.032\)\,m on
ETH and \(+0.069\)\,m on Hotel. These differences are descriptive because
test windows overlap and the benchmark covers five scenes.

\paragraph{Sensitivity to corrupted histories.}
The 0.20\,m noise condition increased mean ADE more than removing and
causally filling the last two positions in each model. DSPIGCN's paired
macro changes were \(\ModelMeanADEChange{dspigcn}{noise_020}\)\,m for noise
and \(\ModelMeanADEChange{dspigcn}{missing_2}\)\,m for missing positions.
For DSPIGCN, the noise penalty was \(+1.719\)\,m on ETH and
\(+2.120\)\,m on Hotel, while the two-missing penalty was \(+0.298\)\,m
on ETH and \(+0.049\)\,m on Hotel.

\paragraph{Runtime and scope.}
The timing evidence uses different hardware and includes owner-specific
preprocessing boundaries. We report timings with their measurement setup
and reserve speed rankings for runs measured on the same host and device.
Architectures, training objectives and budgets also differ, so these
comparisons describe the full implementations. Controlled ablations would
be needed to attribute differences to individual model components.
