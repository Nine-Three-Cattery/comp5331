Project (Group Forming)

Exactly Five or Six (if you want to form a group with 1-4 members, please obtain the instructor's approval. No seven-member group is allowed.)

One group needs to submit the grouping information ONCE only. Please fill in the following information of each member in this link.

  • student ID
  • student name
  • email

After that, our TA, Hong SU, will assign a group ID to your group. You could find your group ID in the group list page (link).

If you do not know some classmates in this class and you want to form a group with other classmates, you have the following 3 options.

  1. Option 1 (Group Forming by Yourself after Our class)
    If you want to find partners by yourself, you could stay after the class to find potential groupmates. 

    Date: 9 Sept (Wed), 11 Sept (Fri) and 16 Sept (Wed)
    Time: 2:50pm

    Date: 12 Sept (Sat)
    Time: 11:50am

  2. Option 2 (Group Forming by our TA)
    If you would like us to form a group for you, please also send an email to our TA, Hong SU (hsuag@connect.ust.hk), with email title "COMP5331: Group Forming Help". In the email, state that you want us to form a group for you. But, it is NOT guaranteed that we will form a complete group for you. Besides, we do not guarantee that the teammates assigned to you in the group must be responsible and must be hard-working. You should need to take a risk on this "random" teammate assignment.

  3. Option 3 (Filling Your Personal Information in an Online Spreadsheet)

    You could fill in the following personal information in the link.
    Then, students who read this information could approach you by sending an email to you. You could also keep reading this list from time to time to contact some potential partners.

    1. Name
    2. Email Address
    3. Project Type
    4. Topic No.
    5. Other Information
